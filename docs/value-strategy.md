# WFM Labs Hub — Value Strategy

## One-Liner
Operational intelligence infrastructure where AI agents and WFM practitioners collaborate on live data.

## The Problem
The world is overflowing with workforce management content — blogs, webinars, whitepapers, LinkedIn posts. None of it is connected to live operational data. None of it has AI agents synthesizing what's happening NOW and engaging practitioners in real-time.

WFM leaders make decisions in the dark:
- "Is this weather event going to impact my Manila operations?"
- "What's the industry benchmark for my workforce topology?"
- "What trends should I be watching in my regions?"
- "How do my peers handle this operational scenario?"

## The Solution: Three Pillars

### Pillar 1: OVIX — Operational Volatility Index
**What:** Real-time operational disruption scoring across 38 global regions and 118 metro hubs.
**How:** 28 data feeds (weather, seismic, disaster, cyber, health, financial, infrastructure) scored every 5 minutes. Composite scoring, volatility tracking, spatial event matching.
**Why it matters:** WFM leaders see the same globe their workforce operates on — with risk overlays, event alerts, and regional intelligence they can't get anywhere else.

### Pillar 2: Intelligence Briefings
**What:** Quarterly synthesized reports + annual private summit.
**How:** AI agents (Beacon) draft analysis from OVIX data, community discussions, and wiki knowledge. Practitioners validate and enhance. Published as subscriber-exclusive briefings.
**Format:**
- Quarterly: 90-minute virtual briefing (live for Team+, recorded for Individual)
- Annual: 1-day hybrid summit (100-200 attendees, subscriber-only)
**Why it matters:** Not regurgitated content — original intelligence synthesized from proprietary data that only this platform generates.

### Pillar 3: Custom WFM Agents
**What:** Purpose-built AI agents trained on WFM methodology, connected to OVIX data, personalized to your operation.
**How:** Subscribers define their workforce footprint (locations, headcount, sourcing, workforce type). Agents calibrate to their profile and deliver personalized intelligence.
**Examples:**
- "Alert me when OVIX scores spike in my regions"
- "Draft my weekly ops briefing using OVIX data for my metros"
- "Monitor my vendor locations for disruption risk"
- "Analyze my forecasting patterns and suggest adjustments"
**Why it matters:** Not generic AI — agents that know WFM, know your data, and take action. The platform does work for you, not just inform you.

## The Flywheel

```
More subscribers
  → more workforce footprint data
  → denser OVIX coverage
  → more precise agents
  → better intelligence briefings
  → more valuable platform
  → more subscribers
```

Each subscriber who fills out their workforce footprint makes the platform more valuable for everyone. This is a network effect, not a content library.
